The demonstration shows acupuncture needles used near the jaw joint capsule and surrounding connective tissue. Scott Popp calls this capsule and fascial needling and distinguishes it from dry needling in the accompanying notes.

The video describes one patient’s experience. An account of an individual case cannot tell a prospective patient how likely an improvement would be, how long it would last or whether the technique would suit another cause of jaw pain. This video shows a clinician’s procedure. It is included as an explanation of what the original recording contains and supplies no instructions for trying it at home.

The source distinguishes the tissue around the joint from a general description of jaw exercise. Its written introduction and the short spoken passage both describe the kind of contact Scott intends. The source is not a comparison of needling methods or evidence that they lead to the same results.
