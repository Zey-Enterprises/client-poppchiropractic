Work hardening in this example means training for the physical demands of a particular job. The video follows a firefighter recovering from injury and shows a session built around lifting, carrying, pulling and climbing.

The example helps explain the distinction between general strengthening and practicing movements chosen for a particular occupation. It shows how Scott considers the end task when thinking about rehabilitation. It does not define a standard program for every occupation, establish how long recovery should take or indicate when a person can return safely to work. The timestamp points to the introduction of the example and its purpose.

The duties named at the start explain why these movements were chosen. Work clearance and recovery timelines require individual assessment.
