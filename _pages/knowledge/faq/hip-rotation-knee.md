The video notes use the hip and knee to explain his interest in the whole movement chain. In the video he demonstrates the large range available at the ball-and-socket hip. His concern is that limited rotation there may change the demand on the knee during a movement that involves the whole leg.

In his separate self-mobilization video he also discusses the hip rotators and tension people notice along the outer thigh. These sources explain why he may look beyond the site of a symptom. His original description simplifies knee mechanics and makes strong claims about the origin of knee pain. The clips describe his assessment model and should not be used to infer that hip restriction accounts for any individual’s knee symptoms.

In the written notes Scott Popp emphasizes a rotational demand reaching the knee. The hip self-mobilization clip emphasizes the outer thigh and surrounding tissues. These two explanations illustrate different parts of the same assessment idea and can be located separately in the original library.
