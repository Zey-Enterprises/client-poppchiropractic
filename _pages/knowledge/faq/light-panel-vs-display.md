A red-light panel is a dedicated light source. A computer display is designed to make text and images visible, and its lighting varies with the display and settings.

A description of a light’s wavelengths or a vendor’s claim about a screen does not establish a clinical effect. If you are comparing devices, check the manufacturer’s specifications and intended use.
