The “posterior chain” refers here to tissues along the back of the body, including the hamstrings, gluteal region and back. In the seated demonstration, the shape of the lower back is observed as the legs extend and the hamstrings come under tension.

The setup shows how the hip, leg and spine coordinate in one position. A seated observation alone does not establish why someone has back pain or identify a tissue that needs treatment. His video on thoracic posture demonstrates a different goal with a similar seated setup.

In the video the knees are straight and the feet are drawn toward the person. Scott observes how the curve behaves as these other positions change. The comparison is between coordination of several parts in a seated task and how those same parts can move in other tasks.
