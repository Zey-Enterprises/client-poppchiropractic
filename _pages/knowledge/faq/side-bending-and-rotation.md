Side bending changes the angle between the trunk and pelvis in a sideways direction. Rotation changes which direction part of the body faces. The lumbosacral handout combines them in a sequence, but it describes them as different parts of the exercise. The first movement is the side-bending portion over the ball. Another image pairs that position with opposite arm directions.

The handout then reverses those directions on the same side. Having a separate picture for each position is useful because a verbal cue such as “bend” does not identify the arm and leg setup. The answer describes how to read the sequence and does not change its repetitions or prescribe a starting range.
