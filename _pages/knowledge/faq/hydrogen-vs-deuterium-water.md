Hydrogen-rich water contains dissolved molecular hydrogen (H₂). Deuterium-depleted water is described by a lower proportion of deuterium, a form of hydrogen present in some water molecules. These are different properties. A label giving deuterium concentration does not tell you the amount of hydrogen gas dissolved in the water.

A hydrogen-water generator adds dissolved hydrogen. Deuterium-depleted water is sold by deuterium concentration. If comparing products, check which property the manufacturer describes and what its testing measures.
