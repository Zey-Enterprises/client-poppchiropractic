The later video presents gym-ball side bending, or lateral flexion, as a progression used in rehabilitation. It adds rotation to the basic movement.

The earlier two-exercise video uses side bending as one of two examples and pairs it with a back plank. In the later clip, the side-bending movement is the focus. Scott explains why he is interested in opening the joint spaces during the motion. That is his proposed rationale for the exercise. If you are matching a protocol to a video, the version matters: the recording itself distinguishes its intended stage and setup from the simpler version.

The handout is another way to locate the movement, but the later clip is specifically labeled as an update and starts by explaining its place in rehabilitation. The earlier clip is useful for identifying the original, simpler comparison between the plank and lateral flexion.
