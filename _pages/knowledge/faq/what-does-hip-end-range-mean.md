The hip handout uses end range to describe the starting portion of two exercises, close to the end of a leg movement in a particular direction. Its pictures show the leg moving across the body in one setup and toward the other foot from an outward position in the other. The rope provides contact or resistance as shown in each setup.

The label describes where the movement occurs. It does not mean that a larger range or a stronger pull is the goal for everyone. The hip-hike demonstration uses a different setup with the leg supporting weight. These references describe distinct movements even though they are all kept in the hip portion of the library.
