In the video’s written instructions, Scott Popp describes joining three short PVC legs with a connector to make a small tool. He presents it as a way of reaching particular points around the hips and the lower and mid back.

The written instructions accompanying the video caution against overworking the points.

In the video, Scott describes the half-inch PVC and the three-way connector together.

At the start of the video Scott Popp shows the connector and explains that the tool applies pressure to a specific spot. Ask about the technique if you are unsure whether it is suitable for you.
