The meta-analysis Scott links compares observed lifetime across groups assigned to screening or no screening. A small group average does not mean that everyone in the group experienced the same result. People can have different outcomes inside the same group. The authors give an interval around each estimate to show its statistical uncertainty.{% include citation.html label="1" %}

For example, the study’s interval for lifetime gained with colonoscopy crosses zero. That describes uncertainty about the estimated change in overall lifetime within that analysis. It does not tell you whether a test detects a cancer in a particular case or substitute for discussing screening options and risk with the clinician who manages your screening.
