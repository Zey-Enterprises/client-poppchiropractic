**Lumbar flexion** is bending of the lower back. **Compression** is load pressing along the spine. The video notes raise a concern about the two occurring together during the seated leg press and use an upright squat to illustrate a different posture under load, even with body weight alone.

The preference for an upright squat is Scott Popp’s clinical view. The notes give no comparative injury rates or evidence that any one squat variation suits everyone.

The notes give both loaded and unloaded examples of an upright squat. That distinction is easy to miss if the discussion is read only as a comparison between a barbell and a machine. He is comparing a position and a way of bearing load, not specifying a weight or training prescription.
