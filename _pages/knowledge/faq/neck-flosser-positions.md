The basic demonstration shows two stretch positions for the upper trapezius and levator scapulae. The positions change the orientation of the head and shoulder. The printable handout pairs the sequences with photos.

A separate video pairs the tool with a more advanced head-and-shoulder counter-rotation. Viewers looking for the simpler demonstration can start with the basic video or the printable protocol. The two clips use the same tool but have different movements and are best read in their own context. Neither title determines whether a device is suitable for an individual neck or shoulder complaint.

In the basic clip Scott adjusts the position of the head and changes where the horizontal bar contacts the upper shoulder. The corresponding handout gives those positions a more convenient place to review them. The advanced clip uses opposition of directions as its main teaching point.
