When the arm moves, the ball-and-socket joint is one part of the motion. Scott’s rope handout also discusses the shoulder blade moving against the ribs and the position and motion of the thoracic spine. Its anatomy illustration separates these views so readers can tell which structure they are looking at.

The scapular video makes the same distinction in another format by showing where the shoulder blade sits relative to the rib cage. The rope handout then uses an external anchor for a particular exercise setup. That setup is separate from the explanation of the anatomy. The listed mechanisms for healing are Scott’s claims and are not findings established by the illustrations.
