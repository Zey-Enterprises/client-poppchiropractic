Look at changes over days and weeks. Movement and symptoms can fluctuate within a day. The guide also distinguishes a change in pain from a change in what a person can do. An exercise, a walk or getting through a work task gives different information than a symptom rating alone.

The examples in the guide include difficult disc problems. They are not a timeline to use for an unexplained symptom, and a particular number of weeks or months is not promised. Use the guide’s distinction to understand why an assessment can include both a history of symptoms and questions about changes in daily activities.
