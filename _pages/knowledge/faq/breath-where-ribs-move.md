The breathing handout contrasts movement near the breastbone with movement around the lower ribs and abdomen. It describes expansion in several directions at once, including out to the sides and toward the back. Its illustration provides a way to understand why the description refers to more than the belly moving forward.

The handout separates noticing an existing breath pattern from practicing a deliberate breathing exercise. Its claims about decompression, circulation and nervous system effects describe the author’s proposed rationale. The diagrams show the movements the author is referring to. They do not determine whether a direction of rib movement accounts for pain, breathing difficulty or posture.
