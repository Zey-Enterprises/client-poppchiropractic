The thoracic curve is the curve of the mid-back. The video considers the head, shoulders, pelvis and ankles together, then shows a seated stretch involving the upper trunk, shoulder blade muscles and tissues along the back.

The explanation is useful for distinguishing what he calls thoracic posture from a neck position alone. His separate posterior-chain demonstration also uses a seated position, but focuses on the relationship between the legs and the curve of the lower back. Comparing the two recordings shows that similar-looking setups can have different goals. The thoracic clip is a movement demonstration, not an assessment of a person’s spinal curve.

The seated drill follows a comparison of the head, shoulders and pelvis. Its focus is movement around the mid-back. A still image shows position only at one moment.
