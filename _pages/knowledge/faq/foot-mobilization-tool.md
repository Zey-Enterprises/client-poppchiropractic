The foot-tool video introduces a device for contacting the foot and working on movement of the surrounding tissues. The forefoot strengthening video shows active rotation of the forefoot. In the library these belong to different kinds of exercise material even though both concern foot movement.

The published foot-tool protocol shows the tool placement and movement sequence. Use the illustration to identify which practice the source describes. The video title mentions both peripheral neuropathy and plantar fasciitis and makes stronger explanatory claims about symptoms. Those conditions are distinct. The tool demonstration does not establish the cause of symptoms or a treatment effect for either condition.

The handout is useful here because it labels where the tool is placed and describes the order of the passes. That makes it easier to distinguish this protocol from the forefoot drill, which does not organize the movement around passes with that same device.
