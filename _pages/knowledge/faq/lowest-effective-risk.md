We choose the lowest-risk option that can address your problem, taking into account your condition and circumstances. The services guide describes both gentle mobilization and quick-thrust manipulation, which have different risks and benefits.

We also assess factors that may contribute to the problem, including injury, mechanical stress, muscle imbalance, tissue health and lifestyle.

This is why the services page describes more than one kind of care. The same visit might lead to discussion of movement, mobilization or an exercise depending on the person’s circumstances. His first principle is a way of choosing between options, not a claim that one technique can address every condition.
