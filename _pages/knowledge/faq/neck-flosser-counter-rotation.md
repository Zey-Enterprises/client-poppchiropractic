The video shows a more advanced use of the neck flosser. In the demonstration the tool contacts the upper shoulder area while the shoulder moves in one direction and the head turns the other way. The contrast between the two directions is the point of his explanation. The clip focuses on movement shared between the neck and upper trunk.

This differs from the basic video, which shows two stretch positions around the upper shoulder. In the advanced clip Scott describes moving the tissue in the direction of the shoulder as the head turns away. Those details help identify the video you are looking for. They do not provide a reason to add resistance or a tool to a neck exercise without individual guidance.

Watch how the shoulder and head change position in opposite directions. The basic clip shows other positions without focusing on the same paired movement.
