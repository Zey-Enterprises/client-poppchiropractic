The Cochrane review Scott’s article discusses compares offers of general health checks with no offer of those checks. The review found little or no difference in overall mortality across the studied groups. The intervention studied is the general check as a program. It is not a targeted consultation for a new symptom or the follow-up of a known condition.{% include citation.html label="1" %}

The distinction also matters when reading claims about individual screening tests. A trial of a general check asks what happens when a package of assessments is offered. A trial of one test focuses on that test under different conditions. Conclusions in one setting should not be treated as advice to skip a symptom evaluation or an individual follow-up visit.
