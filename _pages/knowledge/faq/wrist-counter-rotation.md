The wrist demonstration looks at movement along the shoulder, forearm, palm and wrist. Turning the arm one way while working the soft tissue in the other is the counter-rotation shown here. It illustrates why an assessment may consider more than the wrist itself.

The video groups wrist, hand, elbow and tendon complaints in its title. That does not establish that they have the same cause or that a particular technique treats all of them. There is also a separate eight-step wrist program. Its lessons are unavailable online. The timestamp below opens the demonstration.

The sequence begins by discussing the wrist and hand, then traces the movement more proximally through the arm. When he comes back to the palm, the change in direction makes sense in context. This broader sequence is what makes the clip relevant to his whole-chain approach.
