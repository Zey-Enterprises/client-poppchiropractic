A disc sits between two vertebrae. The video compares it to a cushion and uses lying, standing and sitting to illustrate how the position of the body changes spinal orientation and loading. This comparison helps explain the concern about spending long periods in one position.

The recording gives a simple ranking for those positions. It leaves out the differences between supported and unsupported sitting, individual posture and the load being carried. Treat the ranking as part of Scott’s explanation. It does not tell you what is causing a particular person’s symptoms. The patient guide gives broader context for how he talks about spinal disorders.

In the recording the comparison follows his description of the disc as part of a moving segment between vertebrae. His sequence is meant to explain why he pays attention to what a person is doing for long stretches of the day, in addition to what happens during an exercise.
