The spinal guides distinguish symptoms that recur from movement changes that can persist after an episode of pain. Feeling pain in the same area cannot by itself identify what structure is involved or show that the original injury has returned.

The guides encourage thinking about what someone was doing around the time of a problem and which movements are difficult now. That information provides more context than the location of pain alone. The original guides mention a single numerical recurrence estimate without establishing the applicable population there. The FAQ does not use that figure as a forecast.
