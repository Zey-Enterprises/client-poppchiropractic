The combined foam roller handout names the shoulder blade, rib cage, low back, hip and outer thigh. Its starting position and movement illustrations are there to identify which region the author is addressing. Compare the picture and the heading before assuming that a roller is used the same way in each exercise.

The lat stretching video focuses on one relationship between the shoulder and trunk. The combined handout has a broader sequence. This is why both references appear in the library. The placement used to explain one exercise does not automatically carry over to a different body area.
