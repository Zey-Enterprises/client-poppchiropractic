Gliding surfaces are tissue layers that move relative to one another. The video uses that concept to explain why assessment can consider tissue movement alongside joint range. “Fuzz” refers to the guest clip by Gil Hedley that appears between segments of Scott Popp’s own commentary.

A ball and foam roller serve as examples in the discussion of mobilization. The appearance of the tissue in the guest clip does not by itself identify the cause of stiffness in a living person or show what those tools accomplish. His written introduction discusses movement and inflammation as factors he considers.

The guest excerpt illustrates the appearance of the tissues. The discussion that follows describes mobilization tools. The recording provides no comparison of treatment outcomes.
