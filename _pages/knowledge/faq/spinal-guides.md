The library contains two written versions of Scott’s explanation of spinal disorders. Each has its own 15-point sequence. The patient guide and the self-treatment edition use different wording, so the library keeps both available as separate readings.

For illustrated instructions on a particular movement, use the exercise protocols. The guides provide context for Scott’s approach.

The patient guide is an overview of spinal problems and recovery. The self-treatment edition covers the same topics with its own wording and emphasis. Start with either guide, and ask about treatment decisions at your appointment.
