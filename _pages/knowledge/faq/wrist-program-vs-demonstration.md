The wrist mobilization video shows how Scott Popp relates wrist movement to the arm and shoulder. The eight-step program is a separate resource. Its lessons and enrollment are unavailable online.

Watch the clip for an overview of the technique. If you are looking for a specific lesson or want to confirm an exercise you were shown, call the practice.
