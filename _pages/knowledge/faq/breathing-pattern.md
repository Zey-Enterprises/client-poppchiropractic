The protocol compares movement near the breastbone with movement around the belly and lower ribs. Its observation exercise uses one hand over the breastbone and one over the belly while breathing naturally.

It also illustrates the sides and back of the lower ribs expanding, so the comparison includes more than visible movement at the front of the abdomen. The illustrated protocol contains the full explanation and a separate practice exercise.

The hand placement is an observation aid. The protocol uses the first few breaths to notice the current pattern before introducing a separate exercise that asks the reader to change it. This is a distinction between observing where movement happens and using the description as a diagnosis.
