The squat demonstration looks at how far the lower leg can travel forward over the foot and how that motion couples with rotation below the ankle. A backward shift during the squat illustrates why the foot and ankle are worth considering alongside the hips.

This recording focuses on how the joints share movement under load. His forefoot strengthening video approaches the foot from another angle and deals with active control of the forefoot. The two videos use different demonstrations and give different things to observe. A backward shift in a squat by itself is insufficient to identify a joint restriction or show what exercise a person needs.

The recording begins with a balance observation and then moves to an explanation of the foot. The ankle is being discussed in a task that also includes the knee and hip. That setup is why the demonstration differs from simply moving the foot while seated.
