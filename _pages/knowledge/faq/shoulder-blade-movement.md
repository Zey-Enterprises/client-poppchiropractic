The shoulder blade, or scapula, moves against the rib cage. The demonstration identifies nearby muscles, including the upper trapezius and levator scapulae, before showing hands-on mobilization.

The recording demonstrates his approach to mobilization and introduces techniques he teaches for use outside the office. His focus on the shoulder blade describes how he approaches the demonstration. It does not identify the cause of every shoulder complaint.

Shoulder blade movement against the rib cage is distinct from movement at the outer shoulder joint. The opening anatomy explanation shows where each occurs.
