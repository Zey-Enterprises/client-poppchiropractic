In the foot mobilization handout, ascending and descending describe the direction of travel along the foot. The headings label the ascending pass from heel toward the forefoot and the descending pass as the return. The handout also distinguishes a calf-pump movement at the forefoot from the movement along the foot.

These are labels for finding the correct portions of the illustrated protocol. They do not describe changes in the height of the patient or identify the cause of a symptom. Scott’s forefoot strengthening material uses a different task, focusing on control of forefoot movement. It is useful to distinguish the two references before discussing either with the practice.
