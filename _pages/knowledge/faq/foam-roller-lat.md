Reaching overhead shows why the demonstration considers shoulder movement together with trunk position. The foam roller is used to show where the latissimus is being addressed.

The printable foam-roller protocol covers several body areas, including the shoulder blade, rib cage and lower back. That protocol and the video have different scopes. The clip explains one muscle-focused example, while the handout organizes several mobilization movements. The video links tension in this muscle to more than one complaint. Having symptoms in both areas does not establish that this muscle is the cause.

The shoulder motion in the clip is the reference point for locating the target. The multi-area handout has a different purpose and includes separate placements as it moves from one body area to another. Both sources use a roller, but the instructions are organized around different movements.
