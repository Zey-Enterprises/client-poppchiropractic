This exercise combines balancing on one leg with moving the arms and legs in opposite directions. It isolates some of the coordination used in gait, including opposing turns of the head and shoulders and of the shoulders and pelvis.

The printed protocol gives the exercise a more descriptive name and provides the reference positions. The movement differs from simply standing still on one leg. In the clip, Scott moves through the turn, finds the middle with the arms extended and continues in the other direction. His phrase “the perfect exercise” is the original video title. The useful distinction is the combination of movement tasks shown.

In the video the midpoint is a recognizable reference position. Scott shows how he moves into and out of it while keeping the balance task in place, which is why the arm position is part of the demonstration rather than a decorative detail.
