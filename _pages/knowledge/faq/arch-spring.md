Think of the arch as a spring that changes shape as weight moves onto the foot and helps the foot transition into the next step. The walking demonstration uses this analogy to discuss shock absorption and pivoting.

The forefoot video focuses on rotation and the role of the small muscles of the foot. Together, the videos illustrate arch movement and active foot control. The fallen-arch video argues against relying on rigid shoes or supports. That reflects Scott Popp’s clinical view. The shape of an arch, or its change during walking, does not settle whether prescribed support is appropriate.

The spring analogy describes how the arch behaves during a step. People with similar-looking arches can move differently.
