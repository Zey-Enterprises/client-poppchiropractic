In this practice, “visceral mobilization” refers to manual work around abdominal organs. The reflux video focuses on the area where the esophagus enters the stomach and describes manual pressure and stretching as part of a proposed approach.

That is a description of the technique and Scott’s explanation for why he uses it. The recording does not establish that manual pressure corrects the mechanism of reflux or that reflux symptoms have one cause. It also supplies no comparison with medical evaluation or treatment. The timestamp identifies where Scott introduces the concept so viewers can distinguish his explanation from the exercise and self-treatment clips elsewhere in the library.

The opening passage contrasts the region and technique with Scott’s treatment of movement around the spine and limbs. He then uses a valve analogy for the junction he is discussing. This is why the video belongs under an explanation of his practice rather than a reflux self-treatment protocol.
