Side bending is the movement described as taking the head toward one shoulder. Turning the face to one side is rotation. The cervical lateral handout divides those into separate illustrated exercises. The distinction helps readers match a word used during a visit to the movement the handout actually shows.

The neck-strengthening video also shows retraction, which moves the head back. These movements may appear together in some demonstrations, but they are not the same action. The labels help you identify the intended movement in a reference. They do not determine how far to move, when to use resistance, or which motion to work on for a specific symptom.
