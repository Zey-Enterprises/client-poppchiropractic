The two gym-ball movements are **lower-back extension**, shown as a held back plank, and **lumbar side bending**, shown as a curl over the ball followed by a return to the starting position.

The video’s written notes give the hold and repetitions for those versions. The separate lumbosacral protocol provides its own illustrated instructions, so use its setup when reading that version.

The demonstration marks the transition from the stationary plank to side bends and shows the lower leg positioned forward and the upper leg back.

The setup also matters when distinguishing the two movements. In the plank portion he uses the ball for support while holding his body in position. At the linked transition he changes the relationship of the legs, then demonstrates moving down and back up. The written source and the recording give different repetition counts for the plank, so the FAQ leaves those counts to the original sources and Scott’s guidance.
