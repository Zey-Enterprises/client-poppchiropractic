Learning techniques to use between visits gives you a way to participate in your recovery. The term *internal locus of control* describes the sense that you can influence your own progress.

Care may include exercise, changes to posture, and ways to adjust positions you use every day. Our goal is to help you manage your condition more independently with only as much in-office care as you need.

Use the exercise handouts to review a technique after you have been shown how to do it. Ask us which techniques fit your situation.
