The guides use conservative care to mean lower-risk care, giving exercise, stretching and hands-on treatment as examples. The practice’s services page explains the difference between gentle mobilization and a quick-thrust manipulation. The guide also separates this kind of care from evaluation and treatment that may involve imaging, injections or surgery.

This is Scott’s way of organizing options for discussion, not a rule that every person must go through the same sequence. A diagnostic test and a treatment answer different questions. Choosing an option depends on the circumstances and the condition being evaluated.
