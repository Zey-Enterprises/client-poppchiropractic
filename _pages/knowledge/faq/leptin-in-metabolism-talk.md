About halfway through the talk, Scott introduces leptin as a hormone involved in sensing stored energy. He uses an example in which the body already has energy in reserve and explains the signal in relation to appetite. By “resistance” he is discussing how the body responds to the signal, rather than using the word as a synonym for how much energy the body has stored.

Later in the recording he contrasts this with insulin resistance and proposes an order in which the two develop. He also links his interpretation to environmental light and other exposures. Those later claims are part of Scott’s proposed explanation. The passage defines terms as he uses them in the talk. It does not establish that a particular exposure caused someone’s weight change, that leptin resistance preceded other changes in their case, or that the recording provides a way to diagnose or treat it.
