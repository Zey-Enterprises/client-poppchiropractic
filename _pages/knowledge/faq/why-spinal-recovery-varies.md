Both spinal guides begin by explaining why Scott discourages comparing your recovery with another person’s. They distinguish the symptom someone reports from how the trouble began and how the person moves now. A position that is hard for one person may not be the position that limits another. The guides also discuss age, sleep, activity and stress as context for recovery.

The useful comparison is between your own symptoms and the activities you can do over time. Scott’s guide describes several possible courses of recovery. Its timelines and recurrence estimates do not predict how long any individual will need care.
