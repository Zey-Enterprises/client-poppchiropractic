In Scott’s gait explanation, the head, chest, pelvis and feet rotate in opposing directions around the body’s vertical axis. He uses the swing forward as an example of expansion and the transition from heel strike toward mid-stance as a return toward contraction.

The Expansion Method page has diagrams and an animation for seeing these opposing movements together. His Deliberate Walking post introduces the swing phase in its written description.

In the demonstration, Scott puts the swing phase in the context of two phases of gait. He calls the forward swing an example of expansion and extension.

The contrast helps explain why Scott describes both an outward phase and a return. He does not depict gait as holding one upright position throughout a step. The illustrations let you compare the chest and pelvis at different points rather than looking only at where the forward foot lands.
