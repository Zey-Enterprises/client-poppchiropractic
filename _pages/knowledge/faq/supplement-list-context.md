Start with the full ingredient label, serving size and directions from the manufacturer. Products with similar names can differ in their ingredients and amounts. An ingredient on a label, a product category or a personal routine cannot determine whether a supplement fits your situation.

If you want to discuss a supplement with a clinician or pharmacist, bring the label and a list of medicines and supplements you already take. That gives them the details they need to discuss potential interactions and whether the product is appropriate for you.
