## Website privacy

Online booking opens JaneApp. This site embeds YouTube videos and Google Maps and links to other websites. Those services can receive information when their content loads or when you visit their sites. Review each service’s privacy information before using it.

Site search runs in your browser. The website’s messaging and newsletter controls do not send your entries at present. Please call [307-237-6669](tel:+13072376669) for questions or to ask how to share patient information securely.

## Clinical privacy

For questions about your health information, see [HIPAA documentation](/patients/#hipaa-documentation) or call the practice for the current Notice of Privacy Practices.
