The intake form asks for the information below. Call [307-237-6669](tel:+13072376669) if online submission is unavailable.

## What the intake form asks for

- **Personal and contact details.** Name, address, birth date, phone numbers, email and employer.
- **Emergency contact.** Name, phone number and relationship to you.
- **Insurance and billing.** Provider, payer ID, claims mailing address, member and group numbers, policyholder details, deductible and copay.
- **Authorizations and agreements.** Release of information for insurance claims, assignment of insurance benefits to the practice, the payment agreement and acknowledgment of the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

If the form does not open, call [307-237-6669](tel:+13072376669).
