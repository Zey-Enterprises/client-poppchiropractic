An Advance Beneficiary Notice of Noncoverage (ABN) helps people with Original Medicare decide whether to receive an item or service when Medicare may deny coverage. If an ABN applies, the practice will explain why and provide the appropriate notice before the service. You do not need to request one automatically before booking.
